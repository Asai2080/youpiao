# Typography Policy

Typography is locked by the selected Style Card, not improvised from the photo.

Use the registered family class, width, stroke contrast, tracking, case, scale, depth mode, required features, and forbidden families. Select one Style only; never borrow typography DNA from another Style.

Adapt long titles in this order:

1. apply the Style's width/condensation rule;
2. adjust tracking within the Style range;
3. recompose line breaks only when the Style allows it;
4. reduce scale last.

Maintain the registered title/hero depth relationship. Preserve readability when a Style permits interlocking, swashes, botanical overlap, or a hero passing through the wordmark. Typography errors are repaired at the typography layer without regenerating preserved subjects.
