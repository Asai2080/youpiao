# Cultural Integrity Policy

Reference images provide Style, not transferable cultural content.

Do not migrate flags, crowns, suns, animals, windmills, national ornaments, religious symbols, official emblems, ethnic patterns, scripts, or regional motifs from a Style reference into a new destination unless the input or reliable evidence supports them.

Preserve culturally distinctive details that are truly present. When identity is uncertain, use neutral Style geometry rather than a guessed cultural motif. Decorative borders and Footer ornaments remain subject to this rule even though they belong to Style DNA.
